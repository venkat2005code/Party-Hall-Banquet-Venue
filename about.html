Our Story

Discover the history and passion behind the city's most distinguished venue.

A Vision Realized

Founded by the illustrious Carter family in 1998, Grandeur was built with a singular vision: to provide a sanctuary of elegance in the heart of the city.

What began as a single historic ballroom has blossomed into a multi-venue estate. Over the decades, we have meticulously restored the original 1920s architecture while seamlessly integrating modern, world-class amenities to serve the needs of today's most discerning hosts.
